THE PROPOSED ARCHITECTURE (not implemented or verified yet)
The user wants to swipe through their own Google Photos photos/videos from an iPhone or desktop without their Mac needing to be on. Swiping is independent from applying Google changes. A hosted, authenticated web app/PWA stores a review batch (cached photo previews or video posters, stable private-API media identifiers and metadata) and keep/remove decisions. Full-size originals remain in Google Photos. Preview ingestion/expiry and full video playback need proof-of-concept; do not imply copied thumbnails automatically provide video playback. Decisions can sync across devices. A Google Photos session on the user's own browser is used only during explicit import or apply operations. The hosted backend never receives Google cookies or Google's anti-CSRF/session tokens. It authenticates the user/device separately.

Top-level story: IMPORT BATCH from Google Photos -> SWIPE ANYTIME in hosted app -> REVIEW QUEUE -> APPLY using active Google Photos browser session -> REPORT per-item results. Left marks for removal; right keeps; undo reverses a local/app decision. A left swipe does not immediately delete. Cloud queue stores pending/applying/succeeded/failed states (these are our proposed state names, not Google API states). Only confirmed success marks completed; failures stay available for retry and ambiguous mutations need reconciliation before retry. Original photos are not removed merely because cached previews are removed.
